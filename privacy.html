Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

This policy explains how ALTER, Styling by Christina (“I”, “me”) collects and uses your personal information when you visit stylingbychristina.com, take the style diagnosis, book a call or send an enquiry.

Who I am

The website and styling services are run by Christina Lawson, a personal stylist based in Lisbon, Portugal. I am responsible for your personal information. You can contact me at hello@christinalawson.com.

What I collect

  • Booking enquiries. When you send the booking form: your name, email address, the package and format you choose, what it’s for, when you’d like to start, and anything you tell me about your wardrobe.
  • Intro calls. When you book the free 20-minute call: the details you enter in the booking calendar, such as your name, email address and chosen time.
  • Working together. If you become a client: the information you share during our sessions, such as your style preferences, sizes, photos of outfits and your wardrobe, and the details I need to invoice you.
  • The style diagnosis. Your quiz answers are processed in your browser to show your result. They are not sent to me or stored.

Why I use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange your call or sessions. This is a step you ask me to take before we agree to work together.
  • To provide the styling service you book. This is necessary to carry out our agreement.
  • To issue invoices and keep financial records. This is a legal obligation.

I don’t sell your information, and I don’t use it for marketing unless you ask to hear from me.

Who helps me handle it

I use a few trusted services to run the website. They only process your information to provide their service to me:

  • Formspree receives booking form submissions and forwards them to my email.
  • Cal.com runs the booking calendar for intro calls and sends your confirmation.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and may log technical data such as your IP address.
  • Google Fonts supplies the website’s typefaces, so your browser connects to Google when the page loads.

Some of these providers are based outside the European Economic Area, including the United States. Where that’s the case, your information is protected by safeguards such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long I keep it

  • Enquiries that don’t lead to working together are deleted within 12 months.
  • Client information is kept while we work together and for up to 2 years afterwards, so I can support you if you come back.
  • Invoices and financial records are kept for as long as Portuguese law requires.

Cookies

This website doesn’t use advertising or analytics cookies. The booking calendar from Cal.com may set cookies it needs to work when you book a call.

Your rights

You can ask me to see, correct or delete the personal information I hold about you, to limit how I use it, to object to it being used, or to send you a copy. Just email hello@christinalawson.com and I’ll reply within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how I’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), at cnpd.pt.

Changes

If I change this policy, I’ll update it here and change the date at the top.
