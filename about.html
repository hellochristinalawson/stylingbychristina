ALTERStyling by Christina

Hello, I’m Christina.

My background in fashion spans everything from working on the shop floor to styling photoshoots and managing ecommerce studios for major fashion brands.

I have a degree in Fashion Textiles, experience in pattern making, and I’ve been sewing my own clothes for years. So I understand clothing from every angle; how they’re designed, constructed, fitted and styled.

Today, I bring that experience to personal styling, helping you make the most of your wardrobe, discover new ways to wear what you own, and put together outfits that work for your lifestyle.

Whether you’re stepping into a new role, finding your personal style or simply want to feel more put-together, I’d love to help.
