    • Recommended budget of at least €500, with no obligation to buy
    • Clothing is paid for by you
Do I have to buy anything?

Your styling fee covers my time, expertise and recommendations. Clothing purchases are always your choice, and outfit plans are based on your preferences, budget and what’s available. For Outfit Builder we recommend a budget of at least €300, and for Complete Style Reset at least €500, but there’s no obligation to buy anything.

Do I have to buy a new wardrobe?

No. Most of my work starts with what you already own. A Wardrobe Edit often means buying less, not more.

Will you make me look like someone else?

No. There’s no house style. My job is to find what already works for you and sharpen it.

Online or in person?

Every package works either way. In person is great for audits and fit checks. Online works with photos and a video call, wherever you are.

Do you do colour analysis?

Not a formal seasonal analysis. I’ll give practical colour direction based on what you wear and love.

Can you alter my clothes?

I’ll tell you what’s worth altering, what it should cost and what to ask for. Alterations are a separate, quoted add-on.
